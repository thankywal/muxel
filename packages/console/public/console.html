Muxel
GitHub

An assistant for your business,
running in your own cloud.

Muxel answers your customers on Telegram and on your website, from your own price list and your own policies. It runs inside your own Cloudflare account, so there is no hosted backend in the middle and nothing to trust anyone else with.

How it works

1 · Deploy

One click puts Muxel in your Cloudflare account, on the free plan.

2 · Teach

Send it your price list or policy. It answers from that, not from a guess.

3 · Connect

Point a Telegram bot at it, or paste one line into your website.

4 · Watch

It answers on its own. Step in whenever you want, hand it back when done.

Deploy it, step by step

  1. Create a Cloudflare account

    The free plan is enough. Confirm the email it sends you.

  2. Create a GitHub account

    You never write code there. It keeps the copy your deployment updates from.

  3. Make two bots with @BotFather

    Send /newbot twice. The first is your private console, the second is the one customers write to.

  4. Get your Telegram id

    Send /start to @userinfobot and copy the number it replies with.

  5. Press deploy

    On the form set ADMIN_BOT_TOKEN to the console bot's token and OWNER_TELEGRAM_ID to your number. Leave the rest alone.

  6. Come back here

    Cloudflare gives you a URL for your deployment. Paste it below and the console opens.
