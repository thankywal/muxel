Muxel is an AI assistant that answers your customers on Telegram and on your website, from your own price list and your own policies. It deploys into your Cloudflare account in about ten minutes, on the free plan, and there is no server of ours anywhere in the path.

That is not a promise about what we would not do with your data. It is an architecture in which we never receive it. When you open the console, it has to ask you for your own deployment's address, because it genuinely does not know.

Nothing to run

No VPS to rent, no server to patch, no computer of yours that has to stay on. Cloudflare runs the code in your account, and it is running whether your laptop is open or not.

Start at $0

Deploying it costs nothing and asks for no card: there is no Muxel account to make and nothing to sign up for. Whatever it goes on to cost is Cloudflare's own bill, in your own account, where you can see it.

The free plan covers it

Cloudflare includes 10,000 neurons a day, which we measured at about 880 replies on Gemma 4. Past that you are on Cloudflare's own pricing, in your own account, and the agent is yours either way.

The runtime is not the thing Muxel charges for. When it does charge, it will be for the parts that need a company behind them: verified Messenger, Instagram and WhatsApp channels, one console over many businesses for agencies, and a hosted tier for owners who would rather not touch Cloudflare, which will be the most expensive one because it is the only one where we can see anything. None of that exists yet. Today there is nothing to buy.

Bring your own cloud

One click deploys Muxel into your account on Cloudflare's free plan. Workers runs it, D1 stores it, R2 holds the files, Vectorize does the retrieval. We never see any of it.
