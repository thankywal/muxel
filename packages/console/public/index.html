Muxel is an autonomous business assistant that answers on Telegram and on your website, from your own price list and your own policies. It runs entirely inside your own Cloudflare account, so there is no hosted backend in the middle and nothing to trust us with.

Deploy to your Cloudflare Open the console

Answers from your own facts

Upload a price list or a policy document. It answers from that, not from a guess.

Telegram and your website

One assistant, two front doors. Generate a chat bubble and paste one line into your site.

Take over any time

Step into a conversation, reply as yourself, hand it back when you are done.

Yours to keep

Your Cloudflare account, your database, your keys. Turn it off and the code is still yours.

Bring your own cloud

One click deploys Muxel into your account on Cloudflare's free plan. Workers runs it, D1 stores it, R2 holds the files, Vectorize does the retrieval. We never see any of it.
